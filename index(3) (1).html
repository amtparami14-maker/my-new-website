Professional Diagnostic Workflow

A good diagnostic process starts with the customer's symptom, followed by inspection, DTC analysis, live data review, testing and confirmation of the repair.

  • Read and record DTCs before clearing them.
  • Check freeze-frame and relevant live data.
  • Compare sensor values with expected operating conditions.
  • Test wiring, power, ground and communication circuits when required.
  • Confirm the repair with a final scan and road test.
